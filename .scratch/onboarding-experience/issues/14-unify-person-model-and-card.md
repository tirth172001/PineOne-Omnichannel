## Question

Today `Owner` (`app/onboarding/business-owners/_owner.ts`) and `Signatory` (`app/onboarding/authorised-signatory/_signatory.ts`) are two separate, incompatible types — Owner has `name` + `ownershipPercent` + `hasDocument` (no designation); Signatory has `name` + `designation` + `isOwner` (no percent, no verification state), and is seeded from disconnected mock data rather than the actual owners entered in the prior step.

Merge these into a single **Person** model carrying: `name`, `designation`, `ownershipPercent`, `isSignatory`, and `verificationStatus` (`unverified` | `verifying` | `verified`). Confirmed scope (per user): verification only ever applies to whichever person is selected as the signatory in [Prototype: Authorised signatory](08-prototype-authorised-signatory.md) — plain owners don't carry their own KYC-verified state. `verificationStatus` moves from `unverified` → `verifying` the moment that person starts [Prototype: Face authentication](09-prototype-face-authentication.md), and settles to `verified` on its success.

Design the **person card** used to render this model (replacing the current separate owner-row and signatory-card treatments in `business-owners/_variant-a.tsx:85-119` and `authorised-signatory/_variant-b.tsx:36-67`): avatar (initials, existing `colorForIndex`/`initials` helpers), name, designation, ownership %, a **"Signatory" badge** when `isSignatory` is true, and a **verified badge** that plays a shimmer transition right after face-auth succeeds, then settles into a checked/verified state.

Update `business-owners` and `authorised-signatory` to read and write this shared Person list (via whatever state-threading mechanism [Architect the persistent cross-step preview panel](15-architect-persistent-preview-panel.md) establishes, or a local shared module if that ticket resolves first) instead of today's disconnected mock data.

## Answer

Built the unified model + card and wired real data threading, resolving ahead of ticket 15 (per this ticket's own fallback clause):

- **`components/onboarding/onboarding-person.ts`** — the `Person` type exactly as specified (`id`, `name`, `designation`, `ownershipPercent`, `isSignatory`, `verificationStatus`), plus a module-scoped shared store (`usePeople`/`setPeople`/`useSignatory`/`setSignatory`/`setVerificationStatus`) built on `useSyncExternalStore`. This is the "local shared module" fallback the ticket allowed: it survives Next.js App Router client-side navigation between `/onboarding/business-owners` → `/onboarding/authorised-signatory` → `/onboarding/face-authentication` (no full reload happens between these routes), but resets on a hard reload or a forced/non-SPA navigation — an explicit, acceptable limitation until ticket 15 architects real persistence.
- **`components/onboarding/onboarding-person-card.tsx`** — the unified `PersonCard`: avatar (initials, existing color-cycling), name/designation, ownership %, a "Signatory" pill badge when `isSignatory`, and a corner badge on the avatar that's hidden when `unverified`, shows an animated spinner when `verifying`, and spring-transitions into a checkmark when `verified` (`AnimatePresence` cross-fade between the two icons — the "shimmer... then settles" the ticket asked for).
- **`business-owners/_variant-a.tsx`** — owner rows now render `PersonCard`; `AddOwnerSheet` gained a Designation field and emits a full `Person`. Donut preview unchanged (still ticket 15's remit), just reads `ownershipPercent` off the shared list.
- **`authorised-signatory/_variant-b.tsx`** — candidates are now the *real* owners from the previous step (`usePeople()`), not the old hardcoded `ownerCandidates` mock; `AddSignatorySheet` also emits a full `Person`. Selecting a candidate calls `setSignatory(id)` directly — `isSignatory` flips immediately on click, so the "Signatory" badge and the letter-of-authorisation preview update live, before "Confirm" is even pressed (matches the user's original ask: badge appears "whenever I am selecting signatory amongst those").
- **`face-authentication/_variant-a.tsx`** — reads the real signatory via `useSignatory()` (the confirm screen's "Are you [name]?" now shows the real selected person, not a hardcoded mock name); calls `setVerificationStatus(id, "verifying")` the moment capture starts and `"verified"` on success, exactly as the ticket specified.
- Deleted the now-superseded `business-owners/_owner.ts`, `authorised-signatory/_signatory.ts`, and `face-authentication/_mock.ts`.

**Found and fixed one real bug while building this:** the store's `useSyncExternalStore` server-snapshot function initially returned a new `[]` literal on every call, which isn't referentially stable and triggered React's "getServerSnapshot should be cached" infinite-loop warning (visible as a live Next.js dev-overlay issue). Fixed by returning a module-level stable empty-array constant.

**Verified end-to-end in-browser:** added two owners (Samuel King 40%, Seol Ali 30%) on business-owners — donut and rows both correct; navigated forward (SPA) to authorised-signatory — both real owners appeared as candidates (not mock data); selected Samuel King — badge and letter updated live; separately tested the "add someone who isn't an owner" path (Priya Nair, CFO) — auto-selected as signatory; confirmed → signed → continued to face-authentication — "Are you Priya Nair?" showed the real name; ran the capture flow to success; used the browser's actual back-navigation (not a forced reload) to return to authorised-signatory and confirmed the avatar now shows the green `bg-success` checkmark badge (verified via DOM inspection, not just visually). No console errors at any point; `tsc --noEmit` clean on every file touched.
