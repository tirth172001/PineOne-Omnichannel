# Map: Onboarding v3 — happy-path polish, login consistency & product-intent capture

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A third revision pass over the Pine One onboarding + login/signup experience that: (a) brings the preview-panel card compositions across onboarding into compliance with the interface cheat sheet's spacing/grouping/alignment rules; (b) strips the injected failure-state branches (Singpass/document verification failure, banking-upload failure) so the flow demos as a clean happy path only; (c) makes the login screen's layout and top bar consistent with the rest of onboarding — visual panel on the right, form on the left, plus an extensible account menu; (d) makes the review-and-sign screen's right panel visually consistent with the rest of onboarding's standard split treatment (green animated panel, not a bespoke static card); and (e) introduces a product/use-case intent capture step on the business-name screen that conditionally drives which downstream fields are required — specifically replacing the unconditional generic shipping-address field with a proper, conditional store-address capture (Maps link, current location, or full manual entry) that only appears when the merchant's selected intent actually needs it. Each ticket lands as real, working code in this repo as it resolves — same convention as the v1 and v2 maps, not a spec-only pass.

## Decisions so far

- [Product/intent taxonomy and field-dependency rules](issues/05-product-intent-taxonomy-and-field-rules.md) — four top-level categories drive both the teaser row and the field-gating rule: In-store devices, Online checkout, Payment links, Subscriptions. The "View more" side panel is a richer illustrated catalog for browsing only (grounded in a real Pine Labs product-catalog reference screenshot — Swipe machines A910/A50/A920, QR devices Mini Pro/Mini, Online payment solutions Payment gateway/Tap to Pay on iPhone; Payment links/Subscriptions to be invented by ticket 06 in the same style) — it does not introduce its own selection mechanism or a separate taxonomy; selection stays at the top-level category via one toggle per category. Only "In-store devices" gates anything: it requires the store-address step; every other category (alone or combined) requires nothing extra, and the rule never keys off the specific product/model within a category.
- [Product-intent capture UI](issues/06-product-intent-capture-ui.md) — new `components/onboarding/product-catalog.ts` (the 4-category data + illustrated catalog groups) and `components/onboarding/product-intent-picker.tsx` (`ProductIntentPicker`: 4 selectable rows — not a grid, too cramped in the shell's 320px column — each a real `<label>`+`<Checkbox>` with a separate "Know more" button opening a browsing-only `Sheet`/`Tabs` catalog). Selection persists in `signup-session.ts`'s extended `selectedCategories` field, readable from `app/onboarding/*` later. Wired into `app/signup/business-name/_client.tsx` as its own stagger group between the name field and Continue; selecting a category is optional, doesn't gate submission. Verified live in the browser — selection, "Know more" pre-filtering, and tab filtering all work; no new `tsc` errors.
- [Cheat-sheet audit of preview-panel cards](issues/01-cheat-sheet-audit-preview-cards.md) — two-tier gap scale (8px within a group, 16px+ between groups) applied to the website/app-link, banking, and owners bodies; nested content boxes changed from `rounded-lg` to `rounded-sm` (concentric with the card's `rounded-2xl` minus its padding inset), the active document-row highlight to `rounded-xl` (shallower inset); storefront photo `<img>` gets a 1px inset black/white-8% ring. Scoped to document/banking/owners/signatory modes only — review-and-sign (ticket 04) and business-name's product-intent cards (ticket 06) untouched.
- [Remove happy-path failure states](issues/02-remove-happy-path-failure-states.md) — deleted `verification.failed` and `banking.step === "failed"` from `OnboardingProfile`, and the whole failure-branch UI (destructive copy, "Try again"/alternate-method buttons, "Contact support" link) from `business-verification/_variant-a.tsx`, `banking-details/_variant-a.tsx`, and `onboarding-preview.tsx`. Both flows now succeed on the first attempt — no more injected first-try failure. `lending-application-flow.tsx`/`pos-onboarding-flow.tsx` failure states (separate flows) untouched.
- [Login layout flip and account menu](issues/03-login-layout-flip-and-account-menu.md) — `app/login/page.tsx`'s split flipped (form left, `AuthVisualPanel` right, matching onboarding's convention); added a `DotsThreeIcon` account menu after the language switcher with a single "Logout" item (`SignOutIcon`, clears `lib/dummy-auth.ts` session, redirects to `/login`), structured to accept future items without rework.
- [Review-and-sign panel consistency](issues/04-review-and-sign-panel-consistency.md) — right column of `review-and-sign/_variant-b.tsx` now sits on the shared `GrainientBackground` (floating white card on top), matching the rest of onboarding's preview-panel treatment. Route stays in `FULL_WIDTH_ROUTES` (its `reviewGroups` data isn't threaded through the shared profile store, so it keeps its own two-column layout rather than moving onto `OnboardingRailShell`). Field-grid spacing tightened to the cheat sheet's 8px/16px scale.
- [Conditional store-address requirement](issues/07-conditional-store-address-requirement.md) — removed `basics.addressText` and the whole "Where should we ship your devices?" block from `business-basics`; new `isInStoreDevicesSelected()` predicate in `onboarding-sequence.ts` (reads `signup-session.ts`) drives both `getActiveRoutes()` (filters `store-verification` out of `SEQUENCE`, replacing the static `TOTAL_SUBSTEPS` with per-call active-route math in `getPosition()`) and `onboarding-preview.tsx`'s `getDocSections()` (filters the "store" accordion section), so route indices and preview sections stay aligned either way. `business-basics`'s Continue now branches straight to `website-app-details` when the step is skipped. Verified live, both branches (with and without "In-store devices" selected) end-to-end in the browser.
- [Store-address capture methods](issues/08-store-address-capture-methods.md) — `store-verification/_variant-a.tsx` now offers Maps link / current location (Geolocation API, coordinates only) / full manual form (8 fields, naming matches `pos-onboarding-flow.tsx`'s precedent) inline via `Tabs`, no side panel. `OnboardingProfile["store"]` gained `currentLocation`/`manualAddress` alongside `mapsLink` — only the active method's field is populated. `onboarding-preview.tsx` gained `hasStoreAddress()`/`storeAddressSummary()` helpers so the preview panel summarizes whichever method was used. Verified live: all three tabs work, preview panel and confirmed-count update correctly.
